How I Work

A pragmatic, iterative process that turns raw data into decisions.

01 · Define

Frame the problem

Translate a business question into a clear analytical objective, defining metrics, scope, and the stakeholders' expectations up front.

02 · Clean

Prepare the data

Ingest, validate, and profile the data. Resolve missing values, outliers, and inconsistencies before analysis.

03 · Analyze

Model & explore

Apply statistics, SQL queries, and machine learning to detect patterns, test hypotheses, and isolate performance drivers.

04 · Visualize

Build the story

Design dashboards and visualizations that highlight the key findings and make complex data intuitive.

05 · Communicate

Drive the action

Deliver clear recommendations with trade-offs, so non-technical stakeholders can act with confidence.
